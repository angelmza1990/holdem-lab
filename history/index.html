WSOP Main Event, 1970 to 2026

The $10,000 No-Limit Hold'em World Championship. The field grew from 7 players to more than 10,000 after Chris Moneymaker, an amateur who qualified online, won in 2003.

Most WSOP bracelets

A bracelet goes to the winner of every official WSOP event, in Las Vegas, online, or at WSOP Europe and the other international series. Every player with six or more.

"Other" counts bracelets won at WSOP Europe, WSOP Asia Pacific and WSOP Paradise. Online events since 2020 are counted as listed by Wikipedia, which had not finished reviewing the 2025 and 2026 online events.

WSOP Europe Main Event

Held in London from 2007, then Cannes, Enghien-les-Bains, Berlin, King's Casino in Rozvadov from 2017, and Prague since 2026. It was not played in 2014, 2016 or 2020.

European Poker Tour main events

PokerStars' tour, running since Barcelona in September 2004. In 2017 it was renamed the PokerStars Championship for one season; it includes the PokerStars Caribbean Adventure in the Bahamas, two online main events, and the lower buy-in EPT Open main events.

World Poker Tour

Founded in 2002, the tour that brought hole-card cameras to television. Its season ends with the World Championship, held at Bellagio until 2013 and at Wynn Las Vegas since the $10,400 open event returned in 2022.
