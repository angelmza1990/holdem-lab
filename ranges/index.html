Reading the grid

Each cell is a class of hands. A pair such as 77 has 6 card combinations, a suited hand such as AKs has 4 and an offsuit hand such as AKo has 12, so offsuit cells weigh three times as much as suited ones. In total there are 1,326 two-card combinations, and the percentages count combinations, not cells.

A partly filled cell is a mixed strategy: the hand takes that action that share of the time. Position drives width: each extra player left to act behind you is one more chance that someone holds a better hand, so ranges tighten from the button to under the gun.

The strength view colours each hand by its all-in equity against one, two or five random hands. It is a raw measure: it ignores position, playability and how often a hand can win without showdown, which is why suited connectors are opened ahead of some offsuit hands with more raw equity.
