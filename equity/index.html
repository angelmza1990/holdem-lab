How to read it

Equity is the average share of the pot a player wins over every possible runout, splitting ties. It is what the hand is worth if all the money went in now; with more betting to come, position and skill decide how much of that equity a player actually realizes.

Against a range, every combination counts by how often it is played: AKo:0.5 holds half the weight of AKs per combination, and combinations that clash with known cards are removed. When the number of possible deals is small enough the result is exact; otherwise it is estimated from random deals and shown with its margin of error (95% confidence).
