Texas Hold'em

Hold'em Lab

Tools for the math of No-Limit Hold'em: which hands to play before the flop, how often a hand or a range wins, whether a bet is worth calling, and how much to bet. Everything is computed in your browser.

Preflop rangesOpening, 3-bet and calling ranges by position on the 13×13 grid, hand strength against random hands, and an editor to paint your own. Equity calculatorHands or ranges for up to six players on any board, by exact enumeration or simulation. Pot oddsWhether to call: the equity you need, the largest bet you can call with your outs or your hand, and the implied odds that would make up the difference. Bet sizingMinimum defence, bluff break-even and value-to-bluff ratios by size, the most profitable size for a read, and geometric sizing to get all in.
