What the list leaves out

Buy-ins. A player who enters fifty $100,000 events a year spends $5 million before cashing once. Two players with the same total can be far apart in profit.

Staking and swaps. Professionals often sell shares of their action or swap percentages with other players, so the person credited with a prize may keep only part of it.

Cash games and online play. Some of the most successful players earn most of their money in private cash games or online, and never show up near the top.

Inflation. Johnny Moss's $160,000 in 1974 would be worth roughly a million dollars today; the list compares nominal amounts.
