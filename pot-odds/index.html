The formulas

Facing a bet B into a pot P, calling risks B to win P + B. The call breaks even when your equity e satisfies

e × (P + B) = (1 − e) × B  ⇒  e = B / (P + 2B)

So a half-pot bet needs 25%, a pot-size bet 33.3% and a 2× pot overbet 40%. Turning it around, the largest bet you can call with equity e is B = e × P / (1 − 2e), and with 50% or more you can call any size.

Implied odds are the extra chips you expect to win on later streets when you hit. If the call alone loses money, it breaks even once those future winnings reach (1 − e) × B / e − (P + B).

With a draw on the flop, use the chance of hitting on the next card unless you are all in: if you miss the turn you will usually face another bet. The two-card figure is right when no more money can go in.
